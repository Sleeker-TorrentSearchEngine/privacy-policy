1. Who we are

Sleeker (the "App") is an Android app for searching and downloading torrents, published by Sleeker Cartoons ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. We follow three principles: keep data on your device, collect nothing optional without your consent, and give you simple ways to delete your data.

2. Information kept on your device

The App stores the following only in its private storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

  • Search history: the searches you run, so the App can suggest recent searches.
  • Saved bookmarks: magnet links and titles you choose to save.
  • Downloads: the files you download, plus their file lists and progress, kept in the App's private storage.
  • Settings: your choices, such as Wi-Fi-only downloads, mobile-data rules and privacy settings.

3. Search and the BitTorrent network

Searching

When you search, the App sends your search terms over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) directly to the public torrent search sites that answer the search. Those sites receive the search terms and your IP address, as any website does, and handle them under their own terms. We never receive your searches, and we never send them to analytics.

Downloading (peer-to-peer)

Sleeker uses the BitTorrent protocol. When you download a torrent:

  • Your device connects directly to other people's devices ("peers") and to trackers. This does not pass through our servers.
  • As with every BitTorrent app, your public IP address and port are visible to the other peers and trackers for that torrent.
  • While a file is downloading, your device also uploads parts of it to other peers; this is how BitTorrent works. Sleeker stops all transfer for a torrent when its download reaches 100%.

Only download content you have the right to download.

4. Background downloads, notifications and permissions

So that large files can finish without keeping the screen on, downloads continue while the App is in the background. To do this, the App uses an Android foreground service (on newer Android versions, a user-started data-transfer job), which always shows a visible notification with the download's progress. You can pause or stop downloads from that notification. Running in the background does not collect any extra information.

5. Optional diagnostics

Diagnostics are off by default. Only if you turn on Diagnostics & telemetry in Settings does the App send limited technical events to Google Firebase Analytics:

  • Events: app and screen use (such as app open and screen view), search and download outcomes, and app-health events (search completed, metadata fetched, download finished, transfers restored or stopped).
  • Details: fixed labels and rough ranges only, such as app version, screen name, "1–3 seconds" or "6–20 results", and technical error codes such as "storage full".
  • Never included: search terms, magnet links, torrent hashes, tracker addresses, file names or file paths. The App blocks these before anything is sent.
  • Standard Firebase data: while diagnostics are on, the Firebase SDK also records standard events (such as session start) and device details (such as device model, Android version, app version and an approximate region that Google derives from the IP address).

You can turn diagnostics off at any time in Settings; collection stops immediately.

6. Optional crash reports

Crash reports are off by default and separate from diagnostics. If you turn on Share crash reports in Settings, the App sends crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics, including crashes in native code. A report contains technical details only: where in the code the crash happened, device model, Android version, app version, processor type and a Crashlytics installation identifier. It never includes your searches, torrent names, magnet links, file names or files. Reports saved before you turned the setting on are deleted, and turning it off stops reporting and deletes reports not yet sent.

7. Advertising and your consent

The free version of the App shows ads from the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): banners, ads among search results, full-screen ads at natural breaks, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for a period without ads.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before any ad is requested. Ads are personalized or non-personalized based on your choice.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices (shown where it applies).
  • The ads SDK may use your device's advertising ID and other device signals as described in Google's advertising policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • If you buy Remove Ads, or while a rewarded ad-free period is active, no ads are shown.

8. Purchases and app updates

Remove Ads purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive only the purchase confirmation needed to turn off ads; we never see your payment details. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in the Play Store.

The App uses Google Play in-app updates to check for and install newer versions through Google Play.

10. Keeping and deleting data

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. There is no automatic deletion timer. (An earlier version of this policy mentioned 90-day automatic deletion; that no longer applies.)

  • Search history: Settings → Clear search history.
  • Saved bookmarks: Settings → Clear saved bookmarks.
  • Downloaded files: delete them from the Downloads or Library screens.
  • Everything: uninstalling the App removes all its private data and files.

Data from earlier versions. Versions of Sleeker before 3.0.0 sent an anonymous installation ID and search logs to our Google Cloud Firestore database. Sleeker 3.0.0 no longer sends any of this, and we are deleting the records stored from earlier versions. If you still use an older version, please update to stop it from being sent.

11. Android backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the App's local database (your search history and saved bookmarks) in your personal Google backup, as it does for other apps. Your diagnostics and crash-report choices are not backed up: after restoring on a new device, both start turned off.

12. Security

Searches and our services use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your device is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep what we handle to a minimum.

13. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
